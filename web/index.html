Agent Shelf PayPal sandbox · product data from Channel3

Shopping agents can’t buy what your listing doesn’t say.

Buyers now ask an AI agent to find and buy things for them. Agent Shelf reads your store the way those agents do, shows which buyer requests you lose and why, fills the gaps with facts only you can confirm, and then lets a real agent buy the product through PayPal.

The demo is Amber & Aura, a public sample store from Shopify’s mock.shop. Any Shopify store with a public product feed works.
